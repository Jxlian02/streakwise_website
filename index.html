Everything for your routine. Nothing more.

Streakwise keeps “Today” as clean as possible. Check off, count or time your habits, and let the app do the maths.

One ring per day

Every day becomes a single ring: how much of today’s plan you completed. Swipe back through the past weeks at a glance.

Streaks that are fair

Streaks count in days and only on the days you planned. A day off doesn’t break them, and today stays open until it’s over.

Clear insights

Your average for the week, 30 days or year, compared with the period before. Plus a calendar and stats for every habit.

Apple Health

Steps and distance count automatically. Nothing to type in, nothing to forget.

Widgets & Live Activity

Check off habits right from your Home Screen. A running focus timer stays on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Smart reminders

A nudge at the time you choose, only on planned days, and never for what you have already done.

Your data stays yours.

Streakwise works fully offline and without an account. Your habits live on your iPhone. No ads, no tracking across apps, no selling of data. Usage statistics are only sent if you agree, and your backup lives in your own iCloud, never on our servers.
